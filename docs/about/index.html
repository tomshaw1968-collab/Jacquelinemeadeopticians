About us

An independent, since 1991.

Exceptional eyecare and exquisite eyewear, from a practice that has put patients first on Bank Street for more than 35 years.

Our story

A quality alternative.

“I opened my practice on Bank Street in July 1991 with simple aims: to be the best optician I could be, and to provide a quality alternative to the faceless multiples that were popping up everywhere.”

More than 35 years later, everything has changed except the name above the door, the desire to provide the best possible eyecare, and Jacqueline's love of doing the job. In 2022 the practice was named Practice of the Year at the Eyecare Awards.

“I take nothing for granted — my team and I work hard every day to improve the service we provide. We have an incredibly loyal patient base, and I'm proud of the number of new patients who come to us every week on the recommendation of existing ones.”

Jacqueline Meade
Optometrist & Owner

Meet the team

The people you'll see.

Every member of our clinical team is registered with the General Optical Council (GOC). You can check any registration on the GOC's online register.

Optometrist & Independent Prescriber

Chris Lynch BSc (Hons) DipTp(IP)

In the practice
Monday & Tuesday
GOC registration
01-27386

Chris graduated in Optometry from Glasgow Caledonian University in 2012 and qualified as an Independent Prescriber in 2016, so he can diagnose and treat many eye conditions right here in the practice. With more than 15 years in eyecare, he has a particular interest in emergency eyecare and OCT retinal imaging, and has mentored pre-registration optometrists.

What Chris enjoys most is helping patients understand their eyes, especially when someone tells him a problem they've lived with for years, or one that used to mean a trip to the hospital, has finally been sorted. Away from the practice, you'll find him out walking his golden retriever, usually with a good coffee in hand.

  • Prescribing for eye conditions
  • Emergency eyecare
  • OCT & retinal imaging
Dispensing Optician

Morven Pollock FBDO

In the practice
Monday & Wednesday
GOC registration
D-16746

Morven has worked in eyecare for 15 years and qualified as a Dispensing Optician through ABDO College in 2017. She's the person to see for varifocals and lens advice, frame styling, children's eyewear and ZEISS MyoCare myopia management, and she has a special interest in low vision.

Morven loves helping patients choose lenses that make everyday life easier, and seeing their confidence lift when they collect their new glasses. “Our close team feels like a family. It's a joy to be part of it.” Her own frame taste is anything a bit different, ideally in a bold colour. Outside work she's out and about with her young son, walking, enjoying a coffee or getting cosy at home.
