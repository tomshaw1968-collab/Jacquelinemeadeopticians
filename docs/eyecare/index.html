Eyecare

Clinical excellence, quietly delivered.

Behind the boutique is a fully equipped clinical practice, led by Jacqueline Meade, Optometrist and owner since 1991, with a team of optometrists who are all qualified Independent Prescribers.

  • i.

    Eye examinations

    Free NHS eye examinations for UK residents in Scotland, and our Enhanced Sight Test with optomap® and OCT.

  • ii.

    Children's eyecare

    Eye tests for all ages, free NHS glasses, ZEISS MyoCare myopia management and Tomato Glasses.

  • iii.

    Emergency eyecare

    Red, painful or injured eye, or a sudden change in vision? We aim to see you the same day.

  • iv.

    Contact lenses

    More than 30 years' fitting experience, with full aftercare from our optician.

  • v.

    Glaucoma

    Monitoring and treatment here in the practice, closer to home than the hospital.

  • vi.

    Dry eye care

    Relief for gritty, burning, watery or tired eyes, with a personal treatment plan.

  • vii.

    Home visits

    Not currently offered — but please get in touch and we'll help where we can.
