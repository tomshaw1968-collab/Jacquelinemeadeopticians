About our lenses
A fully digital ZEISS journey

From your eye test to your lenses.

We've invested in a complete ZEISS ecosystem. Your prescription and measurements travel digitally from the test room to ZEISS — nothing re-typed by hand — so your lenses are made individually for your eyes, your face and your frame.

  1. 01

    Your eye examination

    Your eyes are profiled with the ZEISS i.Profiler plus and your prescription refined on our ZEISS VISUPHOR digital refraction system.

  2. 02

    Sent digitally

    Your prescription is transferred electronically from the test room to our ZEISS VISUFIT 1000 — no hand-copied numbers.

  3. 03

    3D measurement

    VISUFIT captures a 180° 3D image of you wearing your chosen frame, measuring its exact position in front of your eyes.

  4. 04

    Frame scanned in store

    Your frame is digitally scanned here in the practice, so its exact shape is captured precisely.

  5. 05

    Custom-made by ZEISS

    Everything is combined and sent electronically to ZEISS, where your lenses are individually calculated and made for you.

Eyecare

Clinical excellence, quietly delivered.

Behind the boutique is a fully equipped clinical practice — trusted by local GPs and the hospital eye department, with technology you won't find at your doctor's surgery.

  • i.

    The Enhanced Sight Test

    optomap® ultra-widefield imaging of around 82% of your retina, plus an OCT scan — in addition to your free NHS examination. £35

  • ii.

    Same-day emergency care

    As part of the Lanarkshire Eye Network Scheme, we make space to see urgent eye problems the same day.

  • iii.

    Independent Prescriber

    All of our optometrists are qualified Independent Prescribers, able to prescribe treatment for many eye conditions — no trip to the doctor required.

  • iv.

    Glaucoma care, close to home

    Monitoring and treatment in the practice rather than at the hospital.

  • v.

    Dry eye care

    Assessment and a personal treatment plan for gritty, tired or irritated eyes.
