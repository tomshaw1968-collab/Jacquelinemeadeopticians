Prescription sunglasses

Clear vision, in the sun.

If you wear glasses, you don't have to choose between seeing clearly and protecting your eyes. We make up prescription sunglasses — single vision or varifocal — using ZEISS Sun Rx lenses, fitted with the same care as your everyday glasses.

  • Polarised lenses to cut glare from water, snow and wet roads
  • Solid and graduated tints in a choice of colours
  • Mirror finishes

Protect your eyes

More than a look.

Too much ultraviolet light can damage the eyes over time and is linked to cataracts and macular degeneration. Good sunglasses are as much about eye health as style.

  • Choose lenses with 100% UV protection (UV400)
  • Look for the UKCA or CE mark
  • Larger frames and wraparound shapes protect more of the eye
  • Children's eyes are especially sensitive to UV — sunglasses matter for them too
