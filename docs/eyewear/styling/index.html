Personal styling

Frames that are truly you.

Colour and face-shape analysis, so you leave with eyewear that flatters your features and your colouring. Complimentary, by appointment.

Colour analysis

The right colours.

The colour of your frames can brighten your complexion — or drain it. Using a set of coloured drapes, we look at how different tones work with your skin, hair and eyes, and identify the palette that suits you best.

From there, we can guide you towards the frame colours, finishes and acetates that will look wonderful on you.

Face shape

The right shape.

Just as important is the shape of the frame. We look at the proportions of your face — its width, jawline and brow — and suggest the shapes and sizes that bring balance and flatter your features.

Then comes the fun part: trying on pieces from our collection of independent and designer eyewear, with honest advice and no rush.

The perfect fit

Then, precision.

Once you've chosen, we measure you with our ZEISS VISUFIT 1000 3D system and fit premium ZEISS lenses — so your new glasses look as good as they perform.

Styling is complimentary and by appointment only — please call the practice to book, so we can set aside time just for you.
